Automated synchronization QC for dubbed content

DubMetric compares a reference master with a dubbed version and measures where the two drift apart — so sync problems are found and reviewed before delivery, not after.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Reference master + dubbed version.

  2. 2

    Analyze

    DubMetric measures:

    • global timing offset;
    • progressive drift;
    • suspicious synchronization ranges.
  3. 3

    Review

    You receive:

    • timing measurements and timecodes;
    • a PASS / WARN / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE result;
    • a downloadable QC report (PDF + JSON).

What DubMetric checks

Global Offset

Detect when the dubbed track is consistently early or late.

Progressive Drift

Identify synchronization that gradually moves out of alignment over time.

Local Sync Anomalies

Highlight suspicious time ranges that deserve closer QC review.
